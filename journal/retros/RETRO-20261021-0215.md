## Lessons -> edits

- playbook (utterance gate): the quoted base rate must come from the speaker's MOST
  RECENT CONSECUTIVE comparable events (last N calls/rallies, no gaps), or from a
  market-series of closed sibling markets. Transcripts found by searching for the target
  word do not count toward the 2-transcript minimum. Count a hit only when the market's
  resolution scope covers that speaker (moderator or analyst reads count only if the
  rules say "anyone"). Evidence: this bet. Its three hits were all found by keyword
  search, and the two unsearched recent calls were exactly the ones that went unverified.
